# Getting help with Ascent

- **Setting something up, or not sure if it is a bug:** ask in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/Vexx3/Ascent/discussions) or the
  [Ascent Discord](https://discord.gg/TbqyC2hJRH).
- **Getting the kit, or a problem with your purchase:** the
  [Discord](https://discord.gg/TbqyC2hJRH). That is not something an issue here
  can help with.
- **Something is broken:** [open a bug report](https://github.com/Vexx3/Ascent/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).

The [troubleshooting page](https://kiels.dev/Ascent/guide/troubleshooting) and
the Tower Setup plugin's **Setup** tab answer most questions before they need
asking.
